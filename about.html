Dr. Kancharla

Dr. Kancharla uses his extensive experience to provide dental care in a gentle, compassionate, and professional manner. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (D.D.S.) degree from New York University in 2011, where he served on a variety of committees and received honors in orofacial pain management.

He researched the relationship between periodontal disease and diabetes and presented his work at national and international conferences. Following dental school, he continued his education through courses in implantology and cosmetic dentistry.

Before settling in Pittsburgh with his wife and daughter, Dr. Kancharla spent four years in private practice in Chicago and Ohio. Outside the office, he enjoys spending time with family and friends and traveling to different parts of the world.
